How to use it

Build a calculator

Join cards.csv with card-damage.csv to reproduce the in-game damage formula. The worked version lives at vampirecrawler.com/damage-formula.

Check an evolution

evolutions.csv has the weapon, the item and the mana cost in separate columns, so you can filter instead of squinting at a chart.

Track completion

achievements.csv plus unlocks.csv is the full 161 + 182 checklist, with the official wording preserved.

Method and caveats

Values were read from the game and cross-checked against the official Steam build, the in-game compendium, and the public patch notes. Where a number is version-dependent, the snapshot date above is the version boundary. If a patch changes a value, the CSV is wrong until it is updated — that is the trade-off of a dated snapshot.
